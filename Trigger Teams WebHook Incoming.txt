Integration Assumptions
Tenko will push every attendance lifecycle event to a configured Teams or Power Platform webhook.
The webhook URL will be provided by the receiving team and stored in Key Vault.
The webhook requires no authentication.
Tenko treats any HTTP 2xx response as accepted.
The receiving endpoint does not need to return a response body.
Tenko records every outbound push so failed delivery can be retried.
The outbound payload includes complete attendance details, including identifying, free-text, medical, course, location, and custom-label fields.
If the receiver is a classic Teams Incoming Webhook, it can display messages in Teams but cannot directly write Dataverse rows. If the receiving team needs automated Dataverse writes, the endpoint should be a Power Automate flow, Teams Workflow, or Logic App that accepts the payload, writes Dataverse rows, and optionally posts a Teams card.
